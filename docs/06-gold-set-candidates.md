## Evidence conventions

- **Fetched** — the page was opened and its cycle, format, rendering mode, and visible fields were checked.
- **Seen** — an official URL was identified but not opened in this pass; it must not be labeled yet.
- **Gap** — a candidate URL was not confirmed or could not be opened; it must not be labeled.
- **Mirror** — the page is hosted by an institution other than the programme owner. Mirrors remain visible for stale-data testing but require owner approval.

`JS` describes the observed or expected rendering surface, not a legal or technical claim about the site. No page contents are committed.
